01.My story

I’m Hussnain Tariq, based in Bahawalpur, Pakistan. I started in the lab — a Chemistry BS, then an MS — and slowly realised that the tools I most wanted to use were ones I’d have to build myself. Software became the thing I do most, but chemistry never left.

I work as a software and AI engineer with an MS in Chemistry, taking R&D data and turning it into production ML systems for research and startup teams — especially in batteries, hydrogen, and drug discovery. I’ve built RAG chatbots, MCP servers, MLOps pipelines, FastAPI backends, IaC infrastructure, and container-native deployments on AWS and Azure.

I collaborate closely with Rao Ibrar Jamal to deliver end-to-end AI and backend-heavy projects. Our shared philosophy: books build engineers, and strong systems start with algorithms, not frameworks.

Most of my formal learning has been supplemented with a lot of self-study — Stanford’s CS229, CS224n, CS231n and CS230 courses; Harvard’s CS50 series; CampusX for practical ML, DL, and GenAI; DeepLearning.AI’s MLOps track; and Vikash Das’s MLOps work. I read a lot too — Kleppmann’s Designing Data-Intensive Applications and Brikman’s Terraform: Up & Running both shaped how I think about systems.
