01.About me

I started in the lab and moved toward code — and I’ve kept one foot in each world ever since. My MS in Chemistry from The Islamia University Bahawalpur taught me to reason from first principles and respect messy, real-world data.

Today I work as a Full-Stack AI Engineer, taking chemistry and R&D data and turning it into production ML models — the kind that ship in APIs, run in the cloud, and survive contact with real users. I’m especially interested in battery materials, hydrogen systems, and pharmaceutical informatics.

I collaborate closely with Rao Ibrar Jamal to deliver end-to-end AI and backend-heavy projects. Our philosophy: strong systems start with algorithms, not frameworks.
